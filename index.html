Core Waste-to-Value Flow

How ReLoop Closes the Loop

Step 01 →

IDENTIFY

AI recognizes the waste item and checks image quality and open-set validity.

Step 02 →

SEGREGATE

The system routes the item to the appropriate destination, or safely rejects uncertain items.

Step 03 →

REWARD

Verified disposal can earn ReLoop Points credited to the student wallet.

Step 04 →

RECOVER

Suitable recovered material enters the clean recycling stream without cross-contamination.

Step 05 ↺

REPEAT

Recovered value can support the campus reward ecosystem and sustain repeat participation.

Core Product Differentiator

"Most smart bins stop at SORT.
ReLoop continues until VALUE."

AI Identification + Physical Sorting + Verified Disposal = ReLoop's Waste-to-Value System
1. AI Identification Operational

Calibrated ResNet50 classifier with multi-view inference, image-quality checks, and open-set rejection of unknown objects.

2. Physical Sorting Simulated in Web UI

Sorting gate routing and safe reject compartment decisions are simulated in the browser console; microcontroller firmware is maintained in the repository.

3. Verified Disposal Session Verified

Separates recognition from reward issuance: points are only granted after an accepted item completes simulated deposit verification.
